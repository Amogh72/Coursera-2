About our platform

Making dealership discovery simpler

Cars Dealership brings dealership locations and customer experiences together in one convenient place.

Explore locations

Browse dealership details and filter locations by state to find options that suit your needs.

Learn from reviews

Read customer experiences and see sentiment labels that help you quickly understand review tone.

Share your experience

Registered users can contribute dealership reviews and help other customers make informed choices.

This application was created as a full-stack learning project using Django, React, Node.js, and MongoDB.
